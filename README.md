<h1>SleekPad</h1>
<p>SleekPad is a simple and sleek stream deck type of micropad containing 9 buttons in total, 8 switches and 1 rotary switch, it also contains a horizontal knob compared to the usual vertical one, the design also contains programmable RGB and a programmable Display. </p>

<h2>Features:</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Sleek case design on a 25 degree angle</li> <li>128x32 OLED Display</li>
  <li>8 SK6812 MINI-E LEDs</li>
  <li>8 Switches</li>
  <li>1 EC11E Rotary encoder (with switch!)</li>
</ul>

<h2>CAD Model:</h2>
<p>The design is made up of 3 layers, the top, middle and bottom, they all join together with the PCB with 4 M3 Bolts screwing into heatset inserts, the board is on a 25 degree angle to mimic the stream deck designs. </p>
